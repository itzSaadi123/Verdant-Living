About Verdant

We're botanists, designers, and plant-obsessives on a mission to green the world.

Our Mission

Plants belong in every home, not just every magazine.

We started Verdant in 2018 with a single greenhouse in Cornwall and a stubborn belief that beautiful, healthy plants shouldn't be a luxury. Today we partner with 40+ independent nurseries across the UK and Europe to bring you varieties you won't find in any garden centre.
